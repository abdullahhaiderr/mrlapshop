# Reference-site adaptation — Intag Laptops

The owner supplied a full-page screenshot and the public reference URL https://intaglaptops.com/. The reference was reviewed for layout and shopping patterns only.

## Patterns adapted for Mr LapShop

- A slim utility strip above a sticky main navigation.
- A multi-column shop mega menu grouped by brand, laptop type, product condition, accessories and repair/support.
- A short factual trust strip directly after the hero.
- Four large shopping entry cards to help customers find products or services.
- Compact product cards with an image, condition, brand/category, spec summaries, price/availability area and primary CTA.
- Dedicated service and after-sales support content.
- A designed customer-story area that can later host owner-approved reviews and customer videos.
- A store visit section and concise FAQ section near the bottom of the homepage.

## What remains unique to Mr LapShop

- Supplied mascot logo and yellow/navy brand palette.
- Rawalpindi store details, contact number, social links and owner-supplied business scope.
- The three actual conditions: New, Used and Open Box. Refurbished listings are not supported.
- Product prices, exact configurations, availability, warranties and review content are not invented.
- Customer reviews and video testimonials remain placeholders until real content is supplied and the future dashboard moderation/publishing flow is built.

## Explicitly not copied

No Intag product photographs, names, descriptions, prices, promotional terms, review ratings/counts, testimonials, store imagery, green styling or claims are reused.
